Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026.

Daybook is a self-hosted business daybook and invoicing app, run privately by Christian Roberton (Pitchblack Company) on his own home server.

This policy explains how Daybook uses information when you optionally connect a Google account. For questions, or to request deletion of data associated with a connected Google account, email xianroberton@gmail.com.

Google Drive

You can optionally connect Google Drive. Daybook uses the drive.file scope and can access only files Daybook itself creates. Those files are used to store and restore Daybook backups.

Gmail

You can optionally connect Gmail. Daybook uses the gmail.compose and gmail.send scopes only to create draft emails with invoice attachments and to send emails you choose to send. Daybook does not read, scan or store your inbox.

How Google data and tokens are stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on your own Daybook server. They are never sold or shared, and they are not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Daybook's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to revoke access

You can disconnect Google access in Daybook settings. You can also revoke Daybook's access to your Google account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of data associated with a connected Google account, email xianroberton@gmail.com.
